# Proposals

**A proposal shapes an idea before it is planned or built.** It says what the idea is for, what it
is and is not, the smallest version worth having, a rough cut of the versions after it, and the
questions still open. It decides nothing: a proposal opens no ADRs, and its open questions stay
open until the owner accepts it.

A proposal's status moves from **Draft** to **Proposal** (finished, waiting on the owner's decision)
to **Accepted** or **Declined**. Once accepted, its versions go to the [Roadmap](/docs/roadmap/),
and the ADRs and change records it needs are written then and linked from it.
[Project Management](/docs/policies/project-management/) describes the whole path.
