# Evaluations

Every hardware and software item checked against the
[Evaluation](/docs/policies/lifecycle-management/evaluation/) criteria before Deevnet relies on it:
its current verdict, and every evaluation behind it. An item's page shows where it stands now, and
its revisions sit folded beneath it, one click deeper.

---
