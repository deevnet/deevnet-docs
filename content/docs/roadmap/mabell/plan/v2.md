# MaBell Project Plan v2: A Low-Voltage Phone

**Plan:** [MaBell Bluetooth Gateway: Project Plan](/docs/roadmap/mabell/plan/) · **Previous:** [v1: On the bench](/docs/roadmap/mabell/plan/v1/) · **Next:** [v3: Line voltage and a real bell](/docs/roadmap/mabell/plan/v3/)

A real telephone makes and receives calls through the gateway, on a 5V DC loop with no
high-voltage hardware. **Status:** next. Its definition of done and tasks are written when it
becomes current, with what v1 taught.

📄 **Build guide:** [`impl/low-voltage-test-rig.md`](https://github.com/cdeever/esp32-ma-bell-gateway/blob/main/impl/low-voltage-test-rig.md)

## Scope

A cheap corded phone with a tone/pulse switch replaces v1's stand-ins:

- **Call tones:** played into the phone's earpiece over the loop, and judged again for authenticity there.
- **Ringing:** still a piezo or LED; a real bell needs line voltage (v3).
- **Hook detection:** the phone's own hook switch, sensed through the loop current.
- **Rotary detection:** the phone's pulse dial, sensed through the loop current.
- **DTMF detection:** the phone's keypad tones, AC-coupled from the loop into the ADC.
- **Call audio:** through the handset, and how much echo the loop brings.

**Not in v2:** line voltage, a real bell, a vintage phone (v3).
