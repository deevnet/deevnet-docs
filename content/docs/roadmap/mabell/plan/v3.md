# MaBell Project Plan v3: Line Voltage and a Real Bell

**Plan:** [MaBell Bluetooth Gateway: Project Plan](/docs/roadmap/mabell/plan/) · **Previous:** [v2: A low-voltage phone](/docs/roadmap/mabell/plan/v2/) · **Next:** [v4: Boxed and installed](/docs/roadmap/mabell/plan/v4/)

A vintage rotary phone rings and works at line voltage, and the circuit board's line interface is
chosen. **Status:** planned. Its definition of done and tasks are written when it becomes current.

📄 **Build guide:** [`impl/slic-module-build-guide.md`](https://github.com/cdeever/esp32-ma-bell-gateway/blob/main/impl/slic-module-build-guide.md)

## Scope

A SLIC (subscriber line interface circuit) gives the phone a real ~48V battery feed, 600Ω line and
ringing voltage. The AG1171/KS0835F module does this from one 3.3–5V supply; the phone side goes on
screw terminals or a protoboard, since tip and ring carry ringing voltage.

- **Call tones:** through the SLIC's hybrid into a vintage phone's earpiece.
- **Ringing:** a real mechanical bell. The firmware toggles the module's F/R pin at 20 Hz to ring, and handles ring-trip when the phone is answered mid-ring.
- **Hook detection:** the module's SHK signal, which is active-high, so the firmware inverts it.
- **Rotary detection:** pulses read from SHK.
- **DTMF detection:** from the SLIC's receive audio.
- **Call audio:** through the real 2-wire to 4-wire hybrid.
- **Optional:** a discrete HC-5504B SLIC with the LT1684 ring generator, on perfboard. It needs −48V and +12V supplies, a 90V AC ring generator, line protection and a hybrid network.
- **The decision:** which line interface goes on the circuit board, AG1171 module or HC-5504B, recorded with the options considered in the project's design docs.

**Not in v3:** the circuit board and enclosure (v4).
