# Roadmap

Captures **forward-looking intent** that is shared across projects.

Roadmaps are **informational**, not binding contracts.

**It is in six areas.** Infrastructure Automation is the tooling: the substrate's, and what the
site offers tenants. Site Work is operational work on a site: upgrades, hardware, building one.
EdS, MaBell and PiPDP11 are the tenants that live in Deevnet for good. Stratum 1 NTP is a project
for fun, part of no site for good. Each has documentation of its
own, and is tracked here because Deevnet builds, certifies and maintains it. Routine operations,
such as applying a patch, are not roadmap items. Every waypoint reached, with its date, is listed
in [Roadmap Progress](/docs/roadmap/progress/). What the work is for, and how far each aim has come,
is in [Value Objectives](/docs/roadmap/value/).

**A deferred item** is one the owner has set aside until a later year. It keeps its place, its
waypoints and its plan, and shows ⏸ with the year it waits for. It is left out of its area's
progress and the value objectives until it is taken up again.
