# Open Records

What is left before each open record can close: each Proposed
[ADR](/docs/architecture/decisions/) accepted, each planned or in-progress
[change](/docs/changes/) closed, each open [incident](/docs/incidents/) closed. **Each bar counts
the items its record lists as left and as done**, so it measures what the record says, and it
falls when an item is added. Each line links to its items further down this page; each heading
there links to the record.

**The items are read from the records themselves.** An ADR lists them under **To accept** and a
change under **To close**, as a checklist; an incident's are its action and follow-up rows. A
record that is open but lists nothing is marked **⚠ no list**.
