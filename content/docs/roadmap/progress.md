# Roadmap Progress

Every waypoint the roadmap has reached, newest first.

- **Waypoint:** a piece of work finished, often one decision or change.
- **🏁 Milestone:** a waypoint that closes a version, when its definition of done is met.
- **Project:** a roadmap item with every waypoint done, completed on its last.

Each month, quarter and year has a written account; open a line's arrow for the waypoints behind it.
Waypoints reached since an account was written are listed beneath it until the next account. A
date marked **by** is the latest it could have been, from the docs' history (since 2025-12-16).
