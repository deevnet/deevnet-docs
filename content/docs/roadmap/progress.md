# Roadmap Progress

How far the roadmap has come: every waypoint reached, newest first. A waypoint is a piece of work
finished, often one decision or change; the tasks behind it are in each roadmap item's project
plan. A waypoint marked **🏁 Milestone** closes a version: the definition of done its plan set for
that version is met. A date marked **by** is the latest the waypoint could have
been reached, from the documentation's history, which starts on 2025-12-16. Each month, quarter
and year has a written account: open a line's arrow to see the waypoints behind it. Waypoints
reached since the last account are rolled up by roadmap item or area until the next one is written;
those reached after an account was written are listed beneath it, under **Since this account**.
The day, week, month, quarter or year that today falls in is marked **in progress**, with the days
left in it; every other is done. A month, quarter or year counts its waypoints, its milestones and
the projects completed in it (every waypoint of a roadmap item done, the last one then), and the
one in progress says what it is on pace for. The bar under each heading compares that period with
the busiest one shown.
