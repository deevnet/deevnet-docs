# Archived Plans

The plans of finished projects, kept as they were when the work closed. They are not updated.
What a finished project is, and how it is built and checked, is its entry in
[Completed Projects](/docs/completed/).

None are archived now. A finished project's plan stays beneath its roadmap item, which shows it
at 100%; a plan comes here only when the owner archives it.
