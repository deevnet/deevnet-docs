# Projects

**Active project plans, in detail.** A [Roadmap](/docs/roadmap/) item says what is intended and
lists its milestones; once work starts, its plan is written here: scope, reasoning, the work in each
phase, status, and the change records that carry it out.

Plans are working documents and change as the work does, so they are kept out of the site's menu.
Each is reached from its roadmap item, and links back to it. When a project is done, its plan moves
to the [archive](/projects/archive/), its roadmap item is removed, and
[Completed Projects](/docs/completed/) gains a short entry linking to it.
